تواصل مع Englishine
Are prices available now?

Final prices are coming soon. Students can book a placement test or contact Englishine to ask about available plans.

Can I watch all videos immediately?

Video access may depend on the student's stage, subscription, and learning plan. Some lessons may unlock gradually.

Are there free videos?

Yes. Englishine may provide selected free previews and reels, while full curriculum access can require a subscription.

What if my lesson is marked قريبًا?

قريبًا lessons are included in the learning path and will be added as recording continues.

Which plan should I choose?

The best plan depends on the student's stage, level, and goals. A free placement test can help choose the right direction.

Is payment available on the website now?

الدفع الإلكتروني غير مفعّل حاليًا. The page currently explains the available subscription options and access structure.
